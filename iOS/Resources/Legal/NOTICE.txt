Shuttl · 关于与致谢 / About & Acknowledgements

Shuttl 使用了以下开源作品和公开研究成果。感谢所有作者。
Shuttl uses the following open-source works and published research. Thank you to all authors.

一、羽毛球检测模型 / Shuttle detection models
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TrackNetV3 (tracking module weights TrackNet_best.pt and network design)
   Copyright (c) 2024 qaz812345
   Licence: MIT (covers the code and the pretrained model checkpoints)
   https://github.com/qaz812345/TrackNetV3
   Paper: Yu-Jou Chen, Yu-Shuen Wang. "TrackNetV3: Enhancing ShuttleCock Tracking with
   Augmentations and Trajectory Rectification." ACM Multimedia Asia 2023.
   https://doi.org/10.1145/3595916.3626370

2. WASB-SBDT (HRNet badminton weights and network design)
   Copyright (c) 2023 NTT Communications Corporation
   Licence: MIT
   https://github.com/nttcom/WASB-SBDT
   Paper: Shuhei Tarashima, Muhammad Abdul Haq, Yushan Wang, Norio Tagawa. "Widely Applicable
   Strong Baseline for Sports Ball Detection and Tracking." BMVC 2023. https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.05237

3. HRNet (network building blocks used by WASB)
   Copyright (c) Microsoft; Copyright (c) 2019 Leo Xiao; Copyright (c) 2019 HRNet
   Written by Bin Xiao, modified by Bowen Cheng. Licence: MIT
   https://github.com/leoxiaobin/deep-high-resolution-net.pytorch
   https://github.com/HRNet/HigherHRNet-Human-Pose-Estimation

   The released weights of 1 and 2 were trained by their authors on the Shuttlecock Trajectory
   Dataset (TrackNet / TrackNetV2, NYCU CoachAI, https://hackmd.io/@TUIK/rJkRW54cU).
   上述模型权重由原作者在 Shuttlecock Trajectory Dataset（阳明交通大学 CoachAI 团队）上训练。
